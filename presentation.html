The Challenge

Problem Statement
🚨

Critical Rural Healthcare Gaps

  • Severe shortage of medicines & oxygen cylinders
  • No real-time hospital bed availability data
  • Lack of trained medical staff in rural areas
  • Poor infrastructure & road connectivity
  • Resources centralized in cities only
💔

Consequences

  • Delayed treatment = higher mortality rates
  • Patients travel 50–100 km for basic care
  • No emergency routing system exists
  • Zero transparency in resource allocation
  • Government unable to track shortages

Market Landscape

Existing Solutions
🏥

eHospital (NIC / MoHFW)

  • Govt hospital management system
  • Complex, non-mobile-friendly UI
  • No real-time resource tracking
  • Not accessible to patients directly
📋

HMIS / RCH Portal

  • Health Management Info System
  • Data-entry focused, not action-focused
  • No emergency SOS or routing
  • Designed for statisticians, not patients
📱

Aarogya Setu / ABHA

  • Digital health identity for citizens
  • No hospital bed/medicine tracking
  • No offline support for rural areas
  • Requires internet + smartphone literacy

⚠️ The Gap None of Them Fill

No existing solution combines real-time resource tracking + patient self-service + emergency routing + government audit + mobile-first PWA in one unified platform for rural India.

Why Does This Happen?

Root Cause Analysis
🏗️

Infrastructure Failure

  • No digitized inventory in PHCs & CHCs
  • Paper-based stock registers — error-prone
  • No centralized visibility for district officers
  • Hospitals operate in information silos
👨‍⚕️

Human Resource Gaps

  • 1 doctor per 11,082 rural people (WHO)
  • High attrition — doctors prefer urban posts
  • No triage system for emergencies
  • ASHA workers lack digital tools
💰

Supply Chain Breakdown

  • Procurement is manual, slow & opaque
  • No real-time shortage alerts to government
  • Vendor lists not maintained or verified
  • Medicines expire due to poor tracking

🎯 Root Cause Summary

The core problem is lack of real-time digital communication between patients, hospitals, and government — leading to resource misallocation, delayed treatment, and zero accountability in rural healthcare delivery.

Understanding the User

Empathy Map
👁️

SEES

  • Long queues at the only nearby PHC
  • "No beds available" boards at hospitals
  • Villagers travelling 60km for medicines
  • ASHA workers with paper forms, no phones
👂

HEARS

  • "The hospital has no oxygen right now"
  • "You'll have to go to the city hospital"
  • Rumours about medicine shortages spreading
  • News about patients dying due to delayed care
💭

THINKS & FEELS

  • Fear — "Will I get treatment in time?"
  • Helplessness — no way to check availability
  • Distrust of the healthcare system
  • Anxiety about cost of travelling to city
🗣️

SAYS & DOES

  • Delays treatment hoping it improves
  • Calls relatives in city to arrange medicines
  • Uses local unqualified practitioners
  • Wishes for an app to check hospitals

💡 Key Insight

Rural patients don't need a complex system — they need one simple app that answers: "Which hospital near me has a bed and medicine available right now?"

Future Roadmap

Strategies to Test
🤖

AI Chatbot — Symptom Analysis

Integrate a Gemini/GPT-powered chatbot in the patient app. Patient describes symptoms → AI suggests urgency level, nearest suitable hospital, and whether to call 108.

🗺️

Google Maps Hospital Locator

Replace text-based locations with Google Maps API. Show real-time hospital pins, distance, estimated travel time, and route to the nearest hospital with available beds.

⚡

Real-Time Updates (WebSockets)

Replace manual refresh with Socket.io WebSockets. Bed counts, medicine stock, and emergency alerts push instantly to all connected users without page reload.

📦

Production-Level Deployment

Upgrade to dedicated Railway server, add NGINX reverse proxy, SSL certificates, Docker containerization, automated DB backups, and Sentry error monitoring.

📱 Convert to Full Mobile App

Migrate from TWA to React Native or Flutter for a truly native cross-platform app (Android + iOS). Enables push notifications, offline-first sync, and Play Store / App Store distribution.
